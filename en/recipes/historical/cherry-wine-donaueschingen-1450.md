## Buolach's Spiced Cherry Mead (Rottweil, c. 1450)

(translated by u/ralfv)

*New to these? Read the [introduction](/recipes/historical) first: it explains what is left out on purpose and what the yeast of the time could do.*

**Source:** House and medical book, Karlsruhe, Badische Landesbibliothek, Cod. Donaueschingen 792, fol. 94r–94v, c. 1450 (Alemannic, western Lake Constance region).

**Language:** Early New High German (Alemannic), Latin phrase in the heading

**Style:** Melomel (cherry), spiced

### Translation

*To make cherry wine, after Buolach in Rottweil.*

If you want to make cherry wine, take 2 *som* of honey and 4 som of cherry juice that has not fermented. Now put the honey in a kettle and let it come to the boil. Then take a small tub full of the cherry wine, pour it into the honey, and let them boil up together. And as much honey as you put in the kettle, put twice as much cherry wine into a *binte*. And when the honey has boiled up, as written above, pour it into the binte with the cherry wine and let it cool like that. Then put it in a cask.

And if you want the said cherry wine really good, then eight or ten weeks after it has been boiled, take ginger, cinnamon, long pepper, cloves and grains of paradise, one *lot* of each, and of the cinnamon two or three lot, and crush it coarsely and put it in a little bag. Hang it in the cask, weighted with a stone, so that it stays hanging in the middle. And let it hang there ten or twelve weeks. Then you have noble good cherry wine.

### Notes

- **"Cherry wine"** (*kirswin*) is the name of the drink; the base is honey and unfermented cherry juice, 1 to 2.
- ***som*:** a large trade measure; only the ratio matters. ***binte*:** a large wooden vessel for filling casks.
- ***lot*:** a small weight, roughly 15 g. Whether the cinnamon gets 2–3 lot in total or in addition to the first lot is open.
- **No yeast is mentioned:** assume the established cellar yeast of a working cask (see the [introduction](/recipes/historical#the-yeast-cellar-yeast-not-wait-and-see)).
