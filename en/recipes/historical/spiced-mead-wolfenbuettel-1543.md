## Nutmeg and Cinnamon Mead (Ludwig Neer, 1543)

(translated by u/ralfv)

*New to these? Read the [introduction](/recipes/historical) first: it explains what is left out on purpose and what the yeast of the time could do.*

**Source:** *Kunst- und Artzneibuch* (Book of Arts and Medicines) compiled by Ludwig Neer, 1543. Wolfenbüttel, Herzog August Bibliothek, Cod. Guelf. 42.3-5 Aug. 4°, recipe 21.

**Language:** Early New High German

**Style:** Metheglin (nutmeg, cinnamon)

### Translation

*How one shall boil a mead.*

Take a whole nutmeg and 2 *lot* of cinnamon quills (?), all cut small, and put them in 4 *mass* of water, and let it boil until one mass has boiled away. After that take 1 *quärtlein* of clarified honey and put that in too, and let it boil until a *seidlein* has boiled away. Then take it off the fire and let it cool.

### Notes

- **Measures:** 1 *mass* = 4 *quärtlein* = 2 *seidlein*. The size of the mass is unknown here (around 1 to 1.5 litres in southern Germany). ***lot*:** roughly 15 g.
- ***Rorlen*** ("little pipes") is read as rolled cinnamon quills.
- **Ratio:** 4 mass of water boiled down to 3, plus ¼ mass of honey (about 1 : 12), then reduced by another half mass. A light mead.
- **The recipe stops at cooling.** Fermentation is not described; assume the established cellar yeast of a working cask (see the [introduction](/recipes/historical#the-yeast-cellar-yeast-not-wait-and-see)).
