## Green Mead from Young Bees' Honey (Bavaria, mid-15th c.)

(translated by u/ralfv)

*New to these? Read the [introduction](/recipes/historical) first: it explains what is left out on purpose and what the yeast of the time could do.*

**Source:** Two nearly identical Bavarian manuscripts of the mid-15th century: Berlin, Staatsbibliothek, Ms. germ. qu. 1187, recipe 265, and Vienna, Austrian National Library, Cod. 2897, recipe 267.

**Language:** Early New High German (Bavarian)

**Style:** Traditional

### Translation

*Of green mead.*

Take honey from young bees; from it you make a green mead. Do with it as you did with the other; put no brown to it.

### Notes

- **"Green"** most likely means young and pale: light honey and no colouring. Other mead recipes of the time colour the mead brown or red with caramelized honey (see [Hopped Red Mead](/recipes/historical/hopped-mead-nuremberg-1389)).
- **"As you did with the other":** the referenced mead recipe is not the one right before it in either manuscript (that is a sour-cherry sauce). The general mead recipe in the same manuscripts is [Egg-Test Mead with Beer Barm](/recipes/historical/mead-egg-test-bavaria-1450).
